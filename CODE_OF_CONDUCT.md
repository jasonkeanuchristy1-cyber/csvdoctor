# Code of Conduct

We want csvdoctor to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background or
experience level.

**Expected behaviour**

- Be kind, patient and respectful. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.

**Unacceptable behaviour**

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or pull requests.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions
that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for
behaviour they judge harmful.

**Reporting**

Report problems privately by opening a
[security advisory](https://github.com/jasonkeanuchristy1-cyber/csvdoctor/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
which is a good reference for the standards we aim to meet.
